Featured project · Web system

CvSU Bacoor Student Portal

Front-end prototype · live demo available

Students at CvSU Bacoor often check grades, schedules and announcements in different places. This portal concept brings them together behind one login, with separate access for students, faculty and the registrar.

I first mapped every actor and function in a use case diagram, then built the student side as a working prototype. It covers sign-in with credential checking, a grades page that computes the general weighted average, a printable certificate of grades, a weekly class schedule, pre-enrollment with a 24-unit limit, filterable announcements and an editable profile.

Key features
  1. 01

    Secure sign-in

    Log In always includes Verify Credentials, and Reset Password is offered as an extension when a student forgets.

  2. 02

    Grades & certificate of grades

    Per-semester grades with an automatic GWA, plus a print-ready certificate of grades.

  3. 03

    Schedule & enrollment

    A weekly class schedule, a status timeline, and subject pre-enrollment that blocks requests over 24 units.

  4. 04

    Announcements & profile

    Announcements filtered by Academic, Registrar or Campus, and a profile form with validation.

  5. 05

    Role-based design

    Faculty encode grades and view class lists, while the registrar approves enrollment and manages records and accounts.

System design

Use case diagram

The diagram defines who uses the portal and what each of them can do. It became the checklist I built the demo against.

  • StudentViews grades (with an optional printed certificate of grades), class schedule, enrollment status and announcements. Pre-enrolls subjects, manages their profile, and logs in and out.
  • FacultyViews class lists, encodes grades and posts announcements.
  • Registrar / AdminApproves enrollment and manages student records, courses and schedules, and user accounts.

Capstone · title approved

KinderLink

For KinderPlay Café · Agile methodology

A management system for a children's play café that keeps check-in, pickup and food orders safe and organized.

  • QR-based guardian check-in and pickup for every child
  • Delegated pickup: a guardian can name an alternate adult, who must show ID, while the original QR code stays valid
  • Allergen-flagged menu mode at the point of sale
  • Requirements gathered through guardian interviews and Google Forms surveys

My role: research, Chapter 1, and the technical, operational and economic feasibility reports. Passed the title defense in November 2026.

Capstone proposal

CavernTrack

For Gamers Cavern Cafe · Web-based POS & inventory

A point-of-sale and inventory system that helps a gaming café take orders faster and avoid running out of stock.

  • QR table ordering so customers can order from their station
  • Stock depletion forecasting based on sales history
  • End-of-day email alerts with sales and low-stock items
  • Customer-focused questionnaire to validate the design

My role: client interviews, feasibility survey, and the project proposal and scope.
